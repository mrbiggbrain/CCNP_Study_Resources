# CCNP Study Materials

## Overview

These are the resources I personally used to study for the CCNP ENCOR and ENARSI exams. Unlike most people I am probably more heavily leaning on the "Learn it" part of certifications and less the "Pass it" part. This means this list probably goes much deeper then you might expect from a normal study resource list. I am providing this as a more comprehensive list that can be used for additional study materials or refrence materials for new network engineers. 

I have placed ★ characters next to resources I think are especially valuble.
